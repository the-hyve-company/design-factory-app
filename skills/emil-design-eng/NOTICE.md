## What this skill is

This skill is an **adaptation** of the upstream `emil-design-eng` skill: it
follows the upstream structure and guidance (animation decision framework,
easing, timing, springs, press states, review format), with the prose
partially paraphrased and some examples re-illustrated.

It encodes guidance Emil Kowalski has published openly — the skill repository
above and his public writing at https://emilkowal.ski. It does **not**
reproduce paid content from the animations.dev course; the course is
referenced only as the place to learn the craft in depth.
